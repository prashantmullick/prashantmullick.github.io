Health Data Sync - Privacy Policy

What this app does: Health Data Sync copies Google Health data (activity, sleep and health measurement summaries) for a single Google account into that account's own Google Sheet.

Where data goes: data is read from the Google Health API, processed by a GitHub Actions job that performs the copy, and written to a Google Sheet owned by the same account. Running the copy involves Google and GitHub systems. Data is not sent to any other recipient, is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Who uses this app: a single account. The app is not offered to other users.

Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
